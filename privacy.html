This Privacy Notice for Tapioca ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share your personal information when you use our services, including when you visit usetapioca.com or use the Tapioca desktop application.

Tapioca is a desktop application for Windows and Mac that allows users to simulate their iPhone's GPS location without jailbreaking. Users connect their iPhone via USB cable and select a location on a map. Tapioca requires a subscription after a 3-day free trial.

Questions or concerns? Contact us at support@usetapioca.com.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services or contact us. This includes:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords (hashed and stored securely)
  • Contact or authentication data

Payment Data. We may collect data necessary to process your payment. All payment data is handled and stored by Stripe. You may find their privacy policy at https://stripe.com/privacy.

We do not collect sensitive personal information, geolocation data, or any data from third parties.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your personal information to:

  • Facilitate account creation and authentication
  • Deliver and facilitate delivery of our services
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests
  • Send administrative information (subscription updates, policy changes)
  • Fulfill and manage your subscription orders
  • Send marketing and promotional communications (you may opt out at any time)

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On?

We only process your personal information when we have a valid legal reason to do so, including your consent, fulfilling our contractual obligations, complying with legal requirements, or protecting vital interests.

If you are located in the EU, UK, or Canada, additional legal basis requirements may apply. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at support@usetapioca.com.

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Information?

We may share your information in the following situations:

  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale, or acquisition of our business.
  • Service Providers: With Stripe for payment processing and Supabase for database hosting.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your personal information for as long as you have an account with us. When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will delete or anonymize it.

6. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information, including password hashing and secure database hosting via Supabase. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure. You should only access our services within a secure environment.

We do not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 18 years of age. By using the Services, you represent that you are at least 18 years old. If you become aware of any data we may have collected from children under 18, please contact us at support@usetapioca.com.

8. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@usetapioca.com.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly.

9. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

We do not currently respond to Do-Not-Track browser signals, as no uniform standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. We use session storage (not cookies) and do not track browsing activity.
